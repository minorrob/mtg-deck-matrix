The tactile Commander experience,
with the bookkeeping handled.

Drag cards from hand, read the full art, arrange your battlefield, follow phases, assign combat, and make your own decisions. CrankMagic automates legal mana payment and repetitive state tracking while Forge remains the authority for the game.

Visual table

Every board stays readable

Physical-style playmats, card stacks, zoom controls, focus swapping, private hands, and a central life tracker keep the pod legible as boards grow.

Rules engine

Forge resolves the game

Casting, targeting, priority, triggers, combat, commander tax, poison, commander damage, loss conditions, and shuffles remain tied to one authoritative engine state.

AI play

Opponents with adjustable judgment

Difficulty scales how early an AI recognizes engines, loops, exposed threats, and vulnerable opponents. GPT-5 Mini is the efficient default; GPT-5 is available for harder decisions.

Human control

Assistance without autopilot

Contextual card actions and recommended plays reduce friction while keeping attacks, responses, targets, activated abilities, and optional effects in the player's hands.

Deck sources

Evidence
