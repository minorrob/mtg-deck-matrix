CrankMagic

Privacy

Last updated October 14, 2026

The short version. Your library lives in your browser. Nothing about it leaves your device unless you sign in to the cloud library or send it somewhere yourself. CrankMagic has no ads, sells no data, and uses no tracking cookies.

Who runs CrankMagic

CrankMagic is a free, unofficial fan project run by Rob Minor. Questions about this page go to admin@crankmagic.com.

What stays on your device

  • Your decks, cards, collection records, notes and history are saved in this browser's own storage, on this device.
  • CrankMagic keeps a copy of its own files in the browser so it can open offline.
  • You can save a backup file at any time (Menu → Save a backup file) and erase everything, so CrankMagic starts over as it does for a new visitor (Menu → Reset All).

If you sign in to the cloud library

The cloud library is optional and invitation-only. When you use it:

  • Signing in is handled by Cloudflare Access, with your Google account or a one-time code sent to your email. CrankMagic receives your email address, and Google may share your name. CrankMagic never sees your password.
  • Signing in sets a session cookie, used only to keep you signed in.
  • What is stored: your library (as a compressed backup of it), when each copy was saved, and a device label such as "Chrome on Windows". The latest copy is kept, along with the 20 most recent saves; a copy set aside when two of your devices disagreed, or replaced by Reset All, is kept for 30 days.
  • Where: in a database run by Cloudflare, in North America.
  • Who can see it: only you, through CrankMagic. The operator can reach the database only to keep the service running, and does not browse libraries.
  • Deleting it: in CrankMagic, Settings › Delete account erases your cloud library, every saved version of it, and your account, at once. Your device's own copy is yours to keep or clear. To have your sign-in removed as well, or if you cannot sign in, email admin@crankmagic.com.

Visit counts

CrankMagic uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views and measure how fast pages load. It sets no cookies, does not fingerprint your device, and does not follow you to other sites.

Services your browser contacts

  • Scryfall for card data and card images, EDHREC for commander popularity, and Archidekt only when you import a deck from it. Your browser asks them directly, so they see your IP address as any website you visit would. CrankMagic does not send them your library.
  • Cloudflare hosts and protects the site; it processes requests, including IP addresses, to deliver pages and block abuse.

Email

Mail sent to admin@crankmagic.com is forwarded to the operator and used only to reply to you. "Send Feedback" in the Menu opens your own mail app with the page you were on and your screen size filled in; you can change or remove them before sending.

AI features

If you are signed in, you can ask CrankMagic to explain a deck's measured score. When you do, CrankMagic sends Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude AI models, only what that explanation needs: the deck's name, its commander, its card names, and the score and figures the simulator measured. It does not send your email address, your library, your collection or what you paid. Anthropic processes the request to write the explanation and does not use it to train its models. CrankMagic keeps a record of each request (who asked, when, how large it was and what it cost) to hold spending to a limit, and deletes it with your account. AI features are open to everyone signed in to CrankMagic, and each runs only when you ask for it (an AI player, when a table's host seats one). Questions about them go to admin@crankmagic.com.

The Coach. At a table, anyone signed in can ask the Coach about their game. When you ask, CrankMagic sends Anthropic your question and what you can see at that table: your own hand, the board as everyone at the table sees it (including the names players use there), the stack, the game's recent history, and your deck's card list. It never sends another player's hand, the order of any library, your email address, your library or your collection. Anthropic processes it to write the answer and does not use it to train its models. CrankMagic keeps the same record of each request as for other AI features, and deletes it with your account.

AI players on Claude. At a table, its host may seat an AI that plays on Claude, Anthropic's AI model, if they are signed in. At some of that AI's decisions, CrankMagic sends Anthropic the AI seat's own view of the game: its hand, the board as every player at the table sees it, and the choices it has. It never sends a person's hand, the order of any library, or anyone's name: players are named by their seat. Each request is logged under the host's account with what it cost, the day's limits hold it, and each game is capped at 25¢. Anthropic does not use what it is sent to train its models.

The deck advisor. On a deck's page, if you are signed in, you may ask Claude, Anthropic's AI model, to suggest swaps. CrankMagic sends Anthropic that deck's list, each card with its roles, whether you own it, its market price and rank; the cards you own, have ordered, or have on your To buy and Watching lists that could go in it, and, if your AI Coach settings allow it, cards you don't own; the deck's own settings; and the goal you type, if any. It never sends your name or email, what you paid for a card, or another person's library. Each request is logged under your account with what it cost, and the day's limits hold it. A suggestion changes nothing until you save it, and Anthropic does not use what it is sent to train its models.

Children

CrankMagic is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Sharing and profiles

When enabled, sharing makes a deck or library available to the registered players you select, or to all signed-in players if you choose public access. Reviewers can view shared content and use shared decks in Play; Collaborators can edit it. Owners control access. Shared library views exclude purchase prices, private inventory notes, account preferences and backup history.

Your profile name and registered email can appear in the signed-in user search used for sharing. Sharing and invitation emails are sent through Cloudflare from notifications@crankmagic.com. Delivery records are used to report failures and retry messages. Changing your sign-in address requires verification of the new address.
