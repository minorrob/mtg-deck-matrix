## 1. What Rob is after

A Commander table that feels like a living place rather than a web page. One tilted tabletop floats inside an animated world; the seat you are looking at is large and in front, the other three are visible but distant across the back; cards move like physical objects with a touch of magic; the environment, the light at the table's edge and the music react to what happens in the game (an instant on the stack, combat) so the state of play is felt before it is read. The rules engine and the server's authority do not change. This is presentation, interaction and awareness.

Four behavioral planes run at the table. Every feature below serves one of them; a feature that serves none does not belong.

| Plane | What the player is doing | Needs from the UI | Visual signal | Audio |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| My turn | Sequencing lands and casts, moving cards on my mat, committing attackers | Big readable mat, soft-snap placement, hover-to-read, phase track, mana and land-drop state | My mat forward and lit; cards float from library to hand and hand to table | Game bed; combat bed during my combat |
| Others' turns | Watching each card as it is played, reading it, planning against it | Reveal shelf for opponents' plays, Stack module with card images, click any back-row mat to look at that seat, Counterintelligence counts | Back row of three mats; quick camera fade on swap | Game bed, never interrupted by turn changes |
| Instant speed | Interjecting on someone's turn; everyone waits to see what resolves | Priority prompt, the new card on top of the stack, response window clear | Neon edge of the table shifts color; fog vents tint | Instant layer over the ducked bed |
| Combat | Attackers, blocks, damage; the table watches one seat | Declare-attackers lock, per-attacker target, totals before confirm (already server-driven) | Edge and fog shift to the combat tint; attackers lean toward their target; arrows | Combat bed, fast in, gentle out |

## 2. Ground truth (from the reconciliation; details in `RECONCILIATION.md`)

### 2.1 Architecture that constrains every PR

- **The board redraws itself whole.** `crankmagic-board.js` `render()` (:959) rewrites `#cm-board`'s innerHTML on every view; `fit()` (:989) measures and sets CSS variables after every render; `sea()` (:1076) keeps one canvas alive across redraws by holding it in a module variable and re-appending it. **Any canvas you add follows the `sea()` pattern.** A `ResizeObserver` (`watch()`, :1147) already calls `fit()` on resize.
- **Three views plus phone** (`VIEWS` :116; key `cm-board-view`): Table, Focus, Full screen (`fullView()` :593-614), `phoneView()` (:851). Full screen is `.cm-board[data-view=full]` = 44px rail / 1fr center / 210px side (`crankmagic.css:3472`); `.cm-full-center` is a 2fr/3fr top/bottom split (:3480), i.e. 40/60; **`.cm-full-mine{position:relative;display:grid}`** (:3484) is where the stage mounts.
- **Cards are DOM buttons**: `card()` (:354) draws `<button class="cm-bcard" data-action="board-card" data-card=…>` with an aria-label that the browser suite reads. `optionsFor()` brightness (`is-bright`), `peek()` hover (:834), `drawPick()` (:620), `zoom()` (:824), keyboard and the suites all depend on cards staying DOM. **Cards never become canvas sprites.**
- **Seat swap is `focus`** (:130): `actions["board-focus"]` (:1261), `actions["board-rotate"]` (:1262); Full screen draws `players()[focus]` as the big board with the corner "Viewing <name>" (:606-608), which `tests/table-board.mjs:621` pins as `.cm-full-viewing`.
- **The view contract** (`game/room/room.mjs` `view()` :489-506; `state` is `projectFor(state, seat)` from `game/engine/projection.mjs`): `{matchId, seatId, seat, revision, status, result, departures, seats, waitingOn, decision, state, history, continuing?}`. The projection carries, per card: `cardId, name (null when hidden), faceDown, owner, controller, tapped, damage, counters{}, types[], power, toughness, keywords[], commander, commanderKey, token`; per game: `turn, turnPlayerId, priorityPlayerId, phase, players[], combat{attackingPlayerId, defenders[], attacks[{attacker, defender, planeswalker?, blocked, blockers[]}]}, stack[{stackId, abilityId, cardId, name, faceDown, playerId, kind, stage, targets[]}], stackSize, gameOver`. **Oracle text and the type line are not in the view**; the board gets the type line from `recordOf(name)` (:316); rules text is fetched by `C.catalog.details(c)` (called from `crankmagic-app.js:486`, never from the board today; Scryfall, async, cached). `history` is the last 120 lines of `{turn, text, mark?}` worded by `game/room/history.mjs`: "X cast Y", "Y resolved", "X played Forest", "X discarded Y", "Y was exiled", "Rob: 40 → 37 life", "Bolt dealt 3 to Rob", "Maya attacked with A, B", "Rob: Bear blocked Wolf", "Maya won". **No history line for a countered spell and none for poison** (history.mjs:47 drops the from-Stack event). There is no events array; what changed is the diff of two views.
- **Audio** (`crankmagic-audio.js`): `createPlayer` (:89) with `arm()` inside the first `pointerdown` on `#cm-board` (board.js:1327), `play(slug)` (:125), `startBgm(slug, fadeMs=1200)` (:140, one bed at a time, crossfade), `setBusVolume`, `setMuted`; `momentsFor(before, after, {seat, typeLineOf, over})` (:179) diffs two views into sound moments; `bedFor(view, seat)` (:240) returns one slug: victory → combat (any `COMBAT_*` phase) → tension (poison ≥ 7 or commander damage ≥ 14) → `bgm_game_aether_voyage`. The board calls `listen(was, next)` (board.js:207-214) per view. Keys `crankmagic-audio-sfx|bgm|muted`. Index `assets/audio/sound-index.json` (schema `CrankMagicPlayAudio@1`, 91 rows).
- **Lobby** (`crankmagic-table.js`): `views.table` (:227-257) polls `GET /api/tables/<id>` every 2 s; `seatBox(s, t)` (:106-136) draws a seat through `C.seatArt.quadrant()` and `seatFigure()` (`crankmagic-seat-art.js:64,:150`); controls today, in order: Choose a deck / Change deck, Choose mat, Ready (only once a deck is chosen); host on an empty human seat: Invite / New link + Withdraw. Deck picker `table-deck` (:332-348) → `knownCounts()` → `POST /known {names}` (:349-364) → `table-use-deck` → **one** `POST /deck {seatId, deck}` (:377-391). Mat picker (:393-426), 30 mats, key `cm-mat`. Center panel (:137-152) with rules, host Start (`table-start`) and Cancel. Countdown: 10 s, `game/contracts/table-lifecycle.mjs:104` (`t.countdownAt = now + 10000`), host-only start/cancel (`table.mjs:287-298`), blockers listed by `countdownBlockers()` (lifecycle :10-42: "no validated deck", "not ready", "not connected", "invitation sent, not joined yet"). No avatar slot; the color fan is passed `[]` on the cloud lobby (table.js:203).
- **Settings** (`crankmagic-settings.js:21-36`): Account, Appearance (theme, default card size slider, Reduce motion → `preferences.reduceMotion` through `{type:'preferences', values:{…}}`), Prices, Data, About. Board Tools ▾ (`toolsMenu()` board.js:443) has card size, Effects, Music, Mute, Skip/Yield, End, Concede.
- **Notices:** `C.notice(message, isError, {action, stay, undo})` (`crankmagic-app.js:127`) is the app's toast at `#cm-notice` (bottom of the page, `role=status aria-live=polite`, 7 s, optional Undo). **This is the "bottom ticker pop-up" Rob refers to.** Use it.
- **Collection groups** (`collection-model.js`): `{type:'createGroup', groupId?, name, template:'general'|'limited'|'bench'|'trade'}` (:711); `{type:'groupEntries', groupId, entries:[{cardId, quantity}], cards?:[records], replace?, batchId?}` (:738). Committed from the board with `C.commit(cmd, {renderView:false})` (board.js:271). - **Release gate** (`tools/release-pages.mjs`): `PLAY_CLOUD` (:74) lists the Play files; `verify()` (:324-361) refuses a cloud release missing one, any file over 25 MiB, anything under `NEVER` (:68: `game/ tools/ tests/ docs/ design/ prototype/ …`), and any `script-src` beyond `'self' https://static.cloudflareinsights.com` (:339). CSP is `script-src 'self'`. **No CDN scripts; a library is vendored and referenced by path from a shipped file** so `walk()` (:277) carries it. Every served file carries `?v=`; `tests/asset-versions.mjs --update` re-records. `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md:103-107` currently says "no vendored libraries".
- **Tests that pin behavior:** `tests/table-lobby.mjs` and `tests/table-board.mjs` are Playwright against the built staging page with a real in-process `GameTable`; the selectors they depend on are listed in `RECONCILIATION.md` A.7. `table-board.mjs` asserts `.cm-seatboard` inside `.cm-full-mine` and `.cm-full-others` in Full screen (:592, :617-635). `tests/wireframe-conformance.mjs` pins the lobby seat header as an absolute bar (:36-46) and the seat card at 5:7 (:57-60). `tests/feature-wiring.mjs` fails any UK spelling (ceiling 0), any `toLocale*`/`Intl` without `'en-US'`, any size picker of steps (`['S','M','L']`, `data-size=`, a group labeled "size"); sizes are sliders. `tests/data-integrity.mjs:631-656` requires README to name every suite and the count. Browser suites run alone after the rest (`tools/run-suites.mjs`).

### 2.2 The Replit prototype (`docs/reference/replit-play/`; `REPLIT-SALVAGE.md`)

What it is: a frontend-only React prototype of Play home, lobby, game and spectate, driven by recorded views (`src/data/sample-views.json`, 42 real views from the repo's own room, seat s0) and synthetic scenarios, with no server, no card images and no music. Its own `HANDOFF.md` is accurate. It was built against the Replit brief v2 and `.agents/memory/play-scope.md`, both of which Rob's 2026-10-13/14 notes and wireframe supersede on several points.

What it settles for this work (values the executor takes rather than invents; file:line in `REPLIT-SALVAGE.md`):
- **Game screen regions and sizes**: strip 48 px; chairs row 190 px default (110-360); hand strip 168 px default (96-340); right pane 340 px default (260-560); dividers 9 px with a 44×3 px handle; phone = `(max-height: 520px) and (orientation: landscape)`, portrait under 600 px shows a "turn your phone sideways" card.
- **A CSS-only 3D table that works**: `perspective: 900px`, `perspective-origin: 50% 26%`, one plane `rotateX(46deg)` with origin at the bottom edge, plane oversized by −9% on the sides and −12% at the top. This is the fallback if the stage cannot mount, and the horizon reference (26% from the top) for the stage. Its 46° tilt and top/left/right opponent placement are superseded by D3/D4 (back row in thirds, tilt 0-35°, default 18°).
- **Motion mechanics proven readable**: ghost-sprite flights with the real card hidden while the ghost flies and a 700 ms safety release; FLIP registry keyed by cardId and by `{owner}:{Zone}` anchors; easing `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)`; flip at 60% of a flight after a 40% delay; 18° tilt on the way to a graveyard; six flights per view. Durations there (180/220/240 ms) are the lower bound; Section 5.3's longer targets stand.
- **Card HUD**: P/T chip at `bottom 3% / right 4%`, font `width × 0.11`, red `toughness − damage` when damaged; d6 pip dice for counters, size `max(12, width × 0.2)`; a crown badge for commanders; tapped tilt with `transform-origin 50% 60%` and a 220 ms ease.
- **Hand strip**: card width `min((height − 34) / 1.4, 56 × handScale)`, playable cards lifted 8 px, phone fan 5° per card.
- **Lobby countdown copy and behavior** (10 s, 1000 ms ticks, `role=timer aria-live=polite`, "Everyone is ready. The table opens on its own"), and the rule that AI seats are ready by construction. Its viewer-side Cancel and "Start it again" are not carried (1c: host-only Cancel; a re-ready restarts).
- **Play home frame**: `max-width 1400px`, hero grid `1.5fr / 1fr`, hero card with the mat image at 0.7 opacity under a left-to-right gradient, three pill buttons primary / bordered / ghost, history cards "result · date · deck · N turns · seats". 1f builds this frame with the repo's own design tokens, not the prototype's themes or fonts.
- **Settings › Play labels and ranges**: Card size 44-130 step 2, Hand size 1-3 step 0.1, Callout intensity and Sound 0-100, Reduce motion "(also follows your system)"; every size is a slider, as `AGENTS.md` requires.
- **Right-pane tab inventory**: Turn Resources, Panel (card), History with a "Show quiet steps" toggle, Tracker (per-seat life, zone counts, poison, commander damage), Combat (attack rows "→ defender · blocked by A, B | unblocked"), Hand counts; and the stack panel grammar "Stack · N", "{kind} · {player}", "+N more below". These seed 4a's module registry; none of Section 6's Counterintelligence analytics exist in the prototype.
- **Keyboard map**: N / Space the one button, H hand, 1-4 look at a seat, C coach, L cycle layout, ? help, Esc own board, Z zoom; ignored while typing.
- **Fixtures**: `sample-views.json` (history oldest-first; no `token`, no planeswalkers, no loyalty counters; `stack[].targets = [{kind:'object', id}]`) and the synthetic scenario builders (big board, deep stack, damage and amount modes, ended, conceded) are usable as Node/vm fixtures for 2a, 3e and 4b.

What it does not settle: no scene/background concept, no fog, no neon edge, no sound pack, no Watch, no avatar, no oracle text, no targeting arrows as a feature (callouts instead), no canvas. Its callout layer (19 kinds, neon text, one at a time) is not in this plan; Rob's planes use the edge, fog and the reveal shelf.

Precedence when the prototype and this file disagree (D20): this file. Known disagreements: seat placement (prototype: you bottom-right, order 1-2-3-you; here: seat 1 top-left and so on, avatar in the outer corner), control order on the seat card, tilt and back-row layout, the 750 ms rotateZ swing and 360 ms shared-layout walk on seat swap (here: 120 ms fade / swap / 120 ms fade; the prototype's note that a 250 ms walk "read as a cut on a large board" goes in the 3c PR body), and one British spelling in its countdown copy.

### 2.3 What does not exist today (so you build it, not find it)

Perspective or any 3D transform in board CSS (none); a canvas tabletop (only `CrankSea`, a still-frame background); an events array; a ticker (only `C.notice`); a Watch group; avatars; a table-level scene; a stack drawn as cards (`stack()` :670 is a text list in three places); a module registry for the panels (`panel()` :676 and `.cm-full-side` :612 are hard-wired); targeting arrows; a bed pool; an instant layer; ramp times per transition (`FADE_MS` is one number); a chat (the brief's table talk is unbuilt).

### 5.3 Motion beats (from `play-board-motion-reference.mp4`; targets, ease-out unless stated)

- **Arrival from hand** (several at once, or an opening hand): cards enter from the near edge below the mat as a loose group, each rotated a few degrees, airborne with a shadow, landing in a row along the far band over 600 ms, staggered 60 ms. A single draw uses the same path for one card over 450 ms, from the library slot to the hand strip.
- **Place and tidy:** a card lifts (shadow grows), slides to its band over 300 ms with a small overshoot, settles. Several tidy in sequence, not all at once.
- **Tap:** rotate 90° in place over 250 ms while a soft white light ring pulses under the card, expanding and fading over 500 ms with a few sparkle particles. Untap reverses the rotation without the ring.
- **Click (select):** the card rocks once left-right about its vertical axis over 250 ms and takes a highlight ring; it is now the selected card (Rob's "rocks back and forth with highlights").
- **Inspect:** a second click on the selected card (or the existing right-click / long-press `zoom` path) enlarges it in place to ≈ 2.2× with a soft glow border; neighbors do not move; stays until click-away or another selection. Hover (`peek`) remains the quick read.
- **Play from hand:** the card lifts out of the hand strip with a glow border and a short trail of fairy dust, arcs onto the mat over 500 ms, lands with the soft light ring, dust fades over 800 ms.
- **Targeting arrow** and **attack arrows:** as 3e.
- **Attack declaration:** the attacking card leans toward its target.
- **Ready state:** cards the viewer can act on (`is-bright` today) carry a slow-breathing cyan ring under the card and a faint cyan glow on the text box; an idle state, never a flash; off when no longer actionable. The on-board twin of the Playable stats block.
- **Camera seat swap:** fade out 120 ms, swap, fade in 120 ms. No camera motion during any beat.
- **Scope:** the beats belong to the mat in the front position, whoever owns it. Back-row mats never play them; opponents' plays use the reveal shelf.

## 6. Counterintelligence: the full spec

**Purpose.** A threat read of the table from the viewer's seat: what is out there that can hurt me, how much, and which cards make it so, without scanning three distant mats. It serves the "others' turns" plane.

**Scope.** The three seats that are not the viewer's. Public information only: battlefield permanents and their current values as the projection sends them (`types`, `power`, `toughness`, `keywords`, `tapped`, `counters`, `commander`), each seat's `health`, and oracle text when the catalog has it (D21). No hand or library inference.

**Analytics** (each a row: total across the three seats, per-seat breakdown on expand, contributing cards on click):

| Row | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Creatures | count of creature permanents |
| Total power / Total toughness | sums over creatures |
| Highest power / Highest toughness | the value and the card |
| Evasion and combat keywords | one row per keyword, count of creatures carrying it: flying, reach, menace, trample, double strike, first strike, deathtouch, lifelink, vigilance, indestructible, hexproof, ward, annihilator, unblockable ("can't be blocked") |
| Flying power | sum of power over creatures with flying (what the viewer cannot block without flyers or reach) |
| Potential attackers next turn | untapped creatures that could attack now (controlled since the start of their controller's current turn, or haste), with their total power. **The projection carries no arrival turn or sickness flag** (projection.mjs:82-109): PR 4b adds `sick: boolean` to the projected card from the engine state if the state tracks when a permanent came under its controller's control; if the engine does not track it, this row is dropped from 4b and filed in the backlog rather than approximated client-side (a client count from `etb` events is wrong after a reconnect) |
| Commanders on the battlefield | per seat, with commander damage already dealt to the viewer (`health.commanderDamage[commanderKey]`) |
| Planeswalkers | count and total loyalty per seat (assumed key `counters.loyalty`; confirm the counter name in the engine before the fixture is written) |
| Open mana | untapped lands plus mana rocks (artifact with a mana ability in its oracle text, when read) |
| Cards in hand | per seat (`zones.Hand.count`) |
| **Passive negative effects** | permanents whose oracle text carries a triggered or static effect against opponents, one row per category, each listing the cards: **Lose life** (`each opponent loses`, `target opponent loses`, `each player loses`), **Poison** (`poison counter`, keywords `toxic`, `infect`), **Discard** (`each opponent discards`, `target opponent discards`), **Destroy or exile** (`destroy target`, `exile target`, `destroy all`, `exile all`, `each opponent sacrifices`), **Damage to players** (`deals N damage to each opponent`, `to each player`, `to any target`), **Mill** (`mills N`), **Tax and restriction** (`can't attack`, `can't cast`, `costs {N} more`, `unless`). A card whose oracle text is not yet cached counts as **unread**; the row shows "n unread" and a **Read the table** action fetches them one at a time through `C.catalog.details`. Never guess a category from a name. |
| New since you looked | permanents that arrived on a back-row mat since the viewer last inspected that seat (the unseen marker's set) |

**Interactions.**

- Click a row → expands to its contributing cards as `Card name - Player name` lines, sorted by contribution (power, then name).
- Hover a line → the card shown exactly as the board's hover render (reuse `card(c, {where:"peek"})` into `#cm-board-peek`, or `drawPick` in Full screen).
- Click a line → a small dropdown: **Go to Player** (sets `focus` to that seat; the existing camera swap with its fade), **Add Card** (Phase 4c: the Watch group and the `C.notice` confirmation).
- Values update on every view; a changed value flashes once.
- Never a recommendation, never a verb like "should" (D16). The row names are nouns.

**Tests** (`tests/crankmagic-counterintel.mjs`, vm): a fixture of three opponent boards with known creatures, keywords, tapped states, `sick` flags (if 4b adds them), planeswalkers with loyalty, lands, a commander with 9 damage dealt, and four permanents with oracle texts covering each passive category plus one unread; assert every row's total, per-seat split and contributor list; assert "unread" where oracle text is absent; assert a seat with no creatures lists none.

**Assumptions to confirm in 4b before writing fixtures** (not verifiable from the staged copy): the shape of `stack[].targets` entries; the loyalty counter key; that `C.catalog.details(c)` resolves to a record carrying `oracleText` (`CrankCatalog` lives in `card-catalog.js`; the board never calls `details`, `crankmagic-app.js:486` does).

## 8. Definition of done and the handoff

A PR is done when: its red-first test is green and the breakage that made it red is described in the PR body; the suites it touches, `feature-wiring`, `asset-versions` (when a served file changed) and `data-integrity` (when a suite was added) are green locally; `tools/local-ci.sh <head> 0` PASS block is on the PR; one green Actions run of the head; `docs/ACTIVE.md` names the next PR; the PR body states what is proven, with the command, and what is unproven, in those words (WebGL rendering on a real GPU, four real seats, audio on a phone, the reveal shelf at a real table). Merge with a merge commit when done, per Rob's standing instruction.

The final handoff (Phase 5) lists every PR, every decision taken from Section 3 with any departure, every backlog line filed, and the UAT steps Rob runs on staging: load a hundred-card deck and cancel at forty; ready up with a friend and watch the countdown start without a Start button; open Full screen at 1400 wide with 3D on, change the scene, tilt the table, click a back-row mat; cast an instant on a friend's turn and hear the layer and see the edge; attack and see the arrows; open Counterintelligence, click Flying, hover a card, Add Card, and find it in Library › Watched at the table.
