940 records, 16 pages of 60

FrictionCollection · Shop

Sixty rows a page is right for a phone and short for a desk: a library this size is sixteen Next clicks, and the folding bands only help when grouping is on. A page-size choice — 60, 120, all — remembered with the column preferences, keeps the phone default and lets the desktop read the whole thing.

Tiles without art show an empty panel

VisualMy Decks

Two of six tiles carried no image (the commander's art is fetched from Scryfall and was not cached), so the tile is a dark rectangle with text at the bottom. A typographic fallback — the commander's initials in the deck's colours, faint, where the art would be — keeps the grid reading as six cards rather than four cards and two gaps.

Looked at and left alone

  • Deck Lab — the five-step run pane and the disabled-with-reason buttons read clearly; nothing to change.
  • Phone Shop — the compressed booth layout holds at 390 px with the Buy button on every row.
  • Filters panel — twelve controls in a grid; dense but each is labelled and the panel is opt-in.
  • Status fly-out — opens left of the menu with the current rung marked; on a phone it overlays the menu, which is the intended fallback.

Walked on the merged files with the Load Live collection restored. Card art and prices come from Scryfall at runtime and were not reachable from the build environment, which is why art is absent in the walkthrough and not on the live site.
