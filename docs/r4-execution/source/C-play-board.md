## Change

1. **CH-30 Click map (D5, C3, RQ-02, D21).** Single click on a card = inspect / the card menu (as today; it is also the single-tap path to every action on touch). Double click on a card or a stack = activate: a land taps and its mana is available; a card with a tap ability taps and the Actions drawer lists its abilities; a castable card the same; with several options the drawer shows them (nothing is sent before the binding press). Double click on the just-tapped card = untap it (E's untap-again action) until that mana is spent or the tap is undone. Double click on empty board area (not a card) in Full, Table and Focus = focus that opponent's board; My board, a double click on your own board, or Esc (C-A7 order) returns; a selection in progress is kept. Remove the 300ms single-click wait: the first click is non-destructive (menu/inspect), so the second click acts at once. The take-back of a land just played moves to the Undo drawer (CH-31). Double-tap on the phone layout is not required: the card menu row is the path.
2. **CH-31 Undo hover drawer (W14 right).** For a card played this step while `undoable` is true: hovering (or focusing) the card slides a band up from the card's bottom edge, `rgba(0,0,0,.65)`, one `hud-caption`-sized line "↶ Undo", 160ms; it overlaps whatever is below, no row spacing changes; click or Enter rewinds the play and untaps its mana (E's undo); once the card is locked in, no drawer. The same drawer on a stack entry while nothing has responded. "undone" appears in History for every seat.
3. **CH-32 Identical lands stacked (W16 r2).** Identity = `sameKey` (same name and state; printings merge; a copy with an Aura, counters, animation, or a token beside non-tokens sits beside the stack until the difference ends). One stack on the battlefield with a count drawer flush to the top card's bottom edge: "×4 · 3 untapped" (same band as Undo, `hud-caption` × scale). A double click taps the next untapped copy; when any is tapped one copy shows tilted 30° (the tilt is a resting state: ≤160ms to reach it, instant under reduced motion), never 90°, and the stack keeps its shape; all tapped: the top copy tilts and a double click does nothing (tooltip "all tapped"); the stack never splits; the whole stack untaps in the untap step; a dual land asks its color at each tap; every seat's board.
4. **CH-33 Divider (W17 r2).** Between battlefield and lands: a 1px `color-line` with an invisible 8px hit area, another `grip()` (role=separator, arrows, double-click reset, cursor row-resize); each side keeps at least one row; stored beside `cm-board-module-h` per device, per view (D9); the 3D view maps the drag so the line stays under the pointer; applies to a focused opponent's board.
5. **CH-34 Camera menu (DS, W18).** 320px, `color-panel`, `radius-card`, `shadow-pop`; View as a segmented control (Table, Focus, 3D: the current view always named); HUD text segmented (100/125/150/200) writing `--hud-scale` (D1 owns the variable; you own the control); Tilt, Zoom, Cards and the new Spacing sliders with 13px labels and the value beside each; Spacing runs from today's gaps through flush (0, default: D19) to overlap, one value for rows and columns, per device with the camera, applies to opponents' boards; overlap left to right so name and cost stay visible, the hovered or selected card rises, P/T readable on the right-most card at the tightest setting; "Appearance preview · this device" becomes "Looks: only on this device"; Table design keeps the mats (the Study is the default).
6. **CH-35 Focus affordances.** "View AI's board, 40 life" (1345×14) and ⤢ Focus (59×24 at 11px) become 32px+ controls in `hud-caption`; a single click on a Boards-list row focuses that seat.
7. **CH-36 Fixed table colors.** Words over the table on `mat-scrim` in `mat-ink`; card face/back tokens; the Study replaces the green `mat-felt` default (X8).
