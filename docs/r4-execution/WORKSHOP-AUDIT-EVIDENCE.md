## Meaning of the dispositions

- **Verified:** the stated original behavior has a concrete passing check or inspected rendered state. This remains local evidence, with the execution limits below.
- **Preserved baseline reconciliation:** a newer accepted baseline replaced the obsolete surface. The evidence names that behavior rather than restoring the older requested design.
- **Partially verified:** a bounded part is proven; the last column names what is still unproven or unresolved.
- **Open:** the original issue remains, or the available evidence is insufficient to claim it is resolved.
