## Explicit combat policy and corrected proof eligibility

`scenario.mjs` now accepts `blockIfLegal: [blocker, attacker]` with an optional defending `seat`. It requires exactly
named, unambiguous creatures in the current combat and the correct defender. The engine's native `blockers.check`
validates both the complete named-pair declaration and any no-block declaration. Menace can reject a single offered
pair; blocking requirements can also reject no blocks. Neither refusal is silently replaced with another answer.
Compact policy logs distinguish answered and refused decisions, survive later assertion failure, and pass through
both direct runtime proof and the card checker's baseline cache. Old no-policy result/error shapes stay unchanged.

Actual loaded Zephyr Sprite against a 2/2 ground fixture reaches `MAIN2`: with Flying the defender has 39 life and
Sprite survives; without Flying the defender has 40 life and Sprite is in the graveyard. Both games finish combat.
The unchanged scenario fails on final life. `tests/scenario-block-policy.mjs` proves this through the existing
counterfactual machinery; its selected in-memory scenario is not a saved-card addition or whole-directory result.

The audit found historical false credit: saved Serra Angel's Flying removal fails only its offered-blocker assertion
before damage. Its explicit no-block declaration still deals four without Flying. Historical selected 4/4 therefore
does not supply completed-combat Flying proof. Thran Dynamo's missing activation offer likewise proves no produced
mana. Their prior coverage artifacts remain historical; current strict coverage requires a new full measurement.

Offer assertions are now ineligible regardless of ability kind or subject identity. Tags cover explicit action offers;
missing action, attack, block and resolve prechecks; question absence/options/seat/minimum; missing named/count-target
options; unanswered prompts; offered blockers; and the new policy's input/legality prechecks. Keyword presence remains
ineligible. Actual final life, tapping, stats, zones, counters and event outcomes retain their existing eligibility.
This corrects runtime-removal proof classification; journal provenance, strict scenario diagnostics, skipped optional
steps, automatic settle answers, default enforcement and remaining gates still need their separately recorded work.
