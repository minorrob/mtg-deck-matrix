### What was wrong

`max-height:72vh` on a box carrying `aspect-ratio:4/3`. **A height cap on a fixed-ratio box caps
its width as well** — the box shrinks in both directions to honor the ratio. On a tall window the
table stopped well short of the content column while the panel below it ran the full width, which
is exactly what the screenshot showed. The ratio was worth keeping; the cap was not.

The card had a second, separate version of the same mistake: `width:min(28%,320px)` stopped it
growing once the table passed 1143px, so on a wide screen it shrank against its own seats.

### What it is now

The table is `width:100%` and `4:3`. A 2×2 grid divides both dimensions, so **every quadrant is
4:3 at any size** — seats keep their shape as they grow, with no rule of their own. The card is
`width:30%` with `aspect-ratio:4/3`, so it carries a seat's shape and scales with the table.

Measured at 1600×950, against the live host:

| | width | height | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| main | 1369 | — | — |
| table | 1297 | 973 | 1.333 |
| a seat | 647 | 484 | 1.335 |
| the card | 389 | 291 | 1.333 |

The seat went from about 460 wide to 647.

**One thing this surfaced:** giving the card `overflow:auto` turned the decorative wand-and-gear
stamp — which is meant to bleed off the bottom-right corner — into a scroll region, and the card
grew two scrollbars. It is `overflow:hidden` now, so the stamp is clipped by the card's own
corner, which is how wireframe 2b draws it.

### Guards left behind

Three checks in `tests/wireframe-conformance.mjs`, all red before the fix:

- *the table is not height-capped, which would cap its width* — fails on any
  `max-height:<n>vh|vw|px` in the base `.cm-lobby-table` rule while it carries an aspect-ratio.
- *the table's card carries a seat's aspect ratio* — reads both ratios out of the sheet and fails
  if they differ by more than 0.02.
- *the table's card scales with the table* — fails on a `min(%, px)` width, which is the shape of
  the bug that capped it.

### 2.1 / 2.2 — the overlap was arithmetic, not judgment

The card is centered over all four quadrants. It is 30% of the table wide and, at the table's own
ratio, 30% of it tall, so it reaches **30% of a quadrant** in from each inner corner. Nothing kept
the seats out of that block, so a seat's detail column and the AI configurator ran under it and
lost whatever was beneath. Each figure is now inset 32% on its inner side, which makes horizontal
intersection impossible whatever the content does vertically. The bottom row's header bar needed
the same inset — the permutation pass measured it at 10,608px² of overlap after the first fix.

### 2.3 — the mats were mine to get wrong

`crankmagic-sea.js`'s six elements are the animated fills **behind** the seats. The real catalog
already existed at `game/ui/playmats.mjs`: nine entries, images in `game/ui/assets/playmats/` and
`game/ui/assets/rob-playmat.png`, served by the host at `/playmats.mjs` and `/playmats/<name>.png`
(`game/tools/serve-review.mjs` lines 29 and 35). It also owns the store — `saveMatPreference`,
localStorage `crankmagic-playmats-v1` — which is **what the table itself reads**, so the picker I
built offered a choice that would have changed nothing in game.

The picker now lists that catalog with its own images and writes through `saveMatPreference`.
Driven: 10 tiles, 8 with art — Surprise me · Runic cube · Moonlit tree · Golden lotus · Sunlit
familiar · Shadow forest · Mountain horizon · Spirit warrior · Violet bloom · Classic black.

`docs/INDEX-where-things-live.md` is the index that should stop this recurring.
