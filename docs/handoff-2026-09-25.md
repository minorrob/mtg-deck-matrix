## 6. Things learned the hard way

- **Code that needs the app's `C` must be a feature** (`(globalThis.CrankFeatures ||= []).push(function(C){…})`). A bare IIFE threw "C is not defined" the first time it reached `C.card` (#372).
- **Menus and pop-ups are popovers on `<body>`, outside `#matrix-v2`.** The `--v-*` tokens are defined on `:root` too for that reason, and a new token belongs in both.
- **An auto popover is dismissed by the press, before the click.** The dispatcher in `crankmagic-app.js` reads the press, so a second click on a menu's button closes the menu. New menus get this for free if they are `.cm-menu[popover=auto]` opened by an action.
- **Scripts that hold a regex go through a file, not a shell heredoc.** In a heredoc sent through the agent's shell, `\b` arrived as a backspace character (`card-catalog.js`, caught and fixed).
- **Testing a hover means resting a real pointer on the name's words.** Hovering a wide button's center, or leaving the pointer where a scroll happened, tests something no reader does. `tests/uat/first-look.mjs`'s `restOn` does it right.
- **The r3 pack contradicts itself on type.** Its "Type (final)" section wins: Satoshi 900, with Young Serif only on heroes of 48px and up.
- **Cloudflare's edge takes about 20 seconds** to serve a new deploy everywhere. The live walk waits for the version meta first.
- **`release-acceptance` against a static folder needs `UAT_STATIC=1`**, since there is no Worker behind `/api/*`. Measure needs `UAT_LIVE_NETWORK=1`, or it rightly refuses a deck whose cards it cannot read.
- **The Access sign-in page is Cloudflare's**, not ours, and the free plan caps Access at 50 people.
