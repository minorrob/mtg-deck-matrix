# Verdigris & Iron

An old ironworks: blue-gray iron, the green patina copper takes on in the rain.

Calmer and cooler than Heather & Granite. Use it when someone wants the app quieter. Its teal accent sits closer to the owned green than heather does, which is why it is the second skin, not the first; the owned rungs still carry their glyph and word.

A skin of the CrankMagic r4 system: this folder sets colors only. Type, shape, motion and every component are the governing system's (../../readme.md).
