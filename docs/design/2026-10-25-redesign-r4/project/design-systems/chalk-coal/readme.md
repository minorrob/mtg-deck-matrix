# Chalk & Coal

Coal-black slate and white chalk: the high-contrast skin, built for 7:1 and a TV across the room.

The high-contrast skin: every text pair at 7:1 or better, lines at 2:1 or better, edges at 3:1. Use it on a TV across the room, in bright light, or for anyone who wants XAG 102's high-contrast mode.

A skin of the CrankMagic r4 system: this folder sets colors only. Type, shape, motion and every component are the governing system's (../../readme.md).
