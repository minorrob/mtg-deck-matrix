Chalk & Coal

Coal-black slate and white chalk: the high-contrast skin, built for 7:1 and a TV across the room. Every value below is computed from build/spec.py, the same source the token files are written from. Targets: 7:1 for every text pair (the high-contrast skin), 3:1 for marks, edges and the focus ring, 2:1 for the strong line, 1.3:1 for dividers and plane steps. 74 of 74 pairs pass.

In the light register a white control on a near-white panel cannot reach 1.3:1 by fill without graying the page; the control's 3:1 field line carries the plane instead, and that pair is in the table. Status chips set their word in ink and carry the hue in the glyph and the bar, so the owned and ordered hues are measured as marks at 3:1; to buy and remove share the money and required hues and are measured as text.

Signals apart is the oklab distance between two signals for typical vision and the three simulated color vision types; 0.08 is the working floor. A pair under it is still told apart by the glyph and the word, never by hue alone (G9).
