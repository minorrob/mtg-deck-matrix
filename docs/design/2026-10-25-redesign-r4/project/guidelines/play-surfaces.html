CrankMagic r4 · guidelines

Play surfaces

The 3D, Table and Focus views share one seat header, one decision, one HUD text scale and one Camera menu, set on the Study: a walnut table under a warm lamp with every seat on charcoal felt. The Actions drawer holds the decision and the Stack drawer holds what is waiting and what happened; both collapse to a tab so the table can fill the screen. A decision is never hidden: with the Actions drawer collapsed it shows in the center slot.

The table companion

The whole surface at once: Actions on the left (the spell, the step, the target, what will happen, two buttons, then the seats and what the decision can do at each), the table in the middle, the Stack and history on the right. Selection on the table is ink: the chosen seat's mat takes a white ring and a check, never a color.

The Study

The default table. Wood grain, lamp light and felt nap are drawn by the browser from noise, so there is no image to load. The felt is charcoal, never a mana color: every commander sits on it equally, and a player's chosen mat replaces it. Each seat wears its commander's art as its portrait, in a brass ring.

Drawer with a collapse tab

A 32px spine on the drawer's inner edge: rounded at its outer top corner, cut back at 45 degrees, the chevron in a 48px cell at the top. Collapsed, the panel goes and the tab stays at the screen edge with the drawer's name and a count. The tab is a real button with aria-expanded; the focus ring sits inside it.
