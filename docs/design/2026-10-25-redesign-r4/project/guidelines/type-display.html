CrankMagic r4 · guidelines

Display type

Two voices. Cinzel 600, roman capitals at .04em, names places and things: every page title, the deck title and the landing hero. The app name keeps its own CrankMagic wordmark. Lato 700 carries h2, h3, section titles and every figure, so the work stays plain. Sentence-case text in Cinzel sets as capitals and small capitals. The h1 is one token that holds its own clamp: 40px at 1440, 30px at 390. No rule restates it.
