Turn 3 · Palette

Five palettes, each a whole system

Every option carries surfaces, one action accent plus the aether accent, the mana identity tints, the five status hues, a display face paired with Satoshi, radius, panel treatment, and the rules for art, motion, mist and the other theme. 3a Brass & Slate is the workshop (what Gallery wears now, sharpened). 3b Aether & Ember is the spellbook. 3c Felt & Cream is the table, light-first. Two heavier, more masculine cuts: 3d Moss & Iron (green, condensed caps, tight radii) and 3e Steel & Cobalt (blue kept but hardened). Decision: Brass & Slate is dark mode, Felt & Cream is light mode, Bricolage Grotesque throughout; 3a, 3c, 3d and 3e are saved as design systems (3d/3e under design-systems/).
