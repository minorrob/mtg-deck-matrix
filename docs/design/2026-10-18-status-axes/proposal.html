1 · Your request, reframed

You asked for nine changes. Seven of them come from one cause: a single "status" word is used for three different questions (what the copy is for, whether you own it, whether the deck can be played), and each screen works it out separately. 3 · Target state

Three statuses, one function

Each card record answers three questions. You set two of them; the app works out the third. All three come from one function, useOf(record), in the collection model, and no screen works a status out for itself. The stored data barely changes. Today's fields already hold every fact; the only additions are splitting the Sell / Trade flag in two and an optional "would replace" note on a watched card (D8).

Use · what the copy is for

FinalSubstituteReservedBenchSellTradeUnowned

Final and Substitute are the only uses that put a card in the deck and count toward its 100.

Set by you: Change status, Put in deck, Reserve

Ownership · where it is in the buying cycle

WatchTo buyOrderedOwnTo sellTrade

No Canceled. Deleting the card removes it. Sell and Trade are stored once, here, and the Use column shows the same value.

Set by you: Change status, Order, Mark arrived

Play · the system's reading

PlayableLoadingTo load

For a card: in the box; owned but not put in yet; or still to get. For a deck: the box holds 100; owned cards cover the gap; a seat is waiting on a card.

Never set by hand. No control changes it.

8 · Your calls

Eight decisions, each with my recommendation

Reply with the IDs, for example "approve; D2 Trade; D5 keep Finalize". Anything you don't mention, I will build as recommended.

D1

Sell and Trade appear in both your lists

Store them once, as Ownership, and have the Use column show the same value. Two stored copies of one fact would drift apart.

Recommend: store once, show in both.

D2

Today's single "Sell / Trade" flag needs to become two

The backup has no offered copies, but your cloud library may. Each existing offer has to start as one of the two.

Recommend: existing offers start as Trade, and the first Library visit after the update lists them so you can change any to To sell.

D3

Does Watching count toward the Group total?

You said To buy counts toward the Group total and not the Deck total, but you didn't say where Watched counts.

Recommend: yes. The Group total is then every row you see when the Library is scoped to the deck: 114 in your example.

D4

Taking a card from another deck puts a replacement on To buy

I read your Reserve step 2 as covering this case too: the deck you take the copy from becomes one short.

Recommend: ask in the Reserve dialog. "No" leaves that seat Reserved · empty seat until you decide.

D5

"Final" collides with the deck's Finalize button

A deck is currently draft or final, and the button that locks the list says Finalize. Having both "a Final card" and "a final deck" will confuse people.

Recommend: rename the button "Lock list", and call a deck Draft or Locked.

D6

Dropping Bench cards from the deck scope reverses your Sep 14 call

On Sep 14 the reason for keeping them was "what do I already own that could go in it". That is still a useful question.

Recommend: deck scope shows the deck's group only. A "Show Bench cards that could go in" checkbox, off by default, brings the old view back on demand.

D7

Play status and the deck stages

Deck tiles show Defining, Building, Playable and Complete today.

Recommend: tiles show Play status (Playable, Loading, To load). "Complete" stays as a badge for a deck with nothing Reserved. Defining becomes To load with the note "list not locked".

D8

A watched card can name the card it would replace

Your example says the 4 watched cards are "replacements for certain cards in the 100".

Recommend: an optional "would replace" field on a watched card, shown as a note in The 100. When you reserve it, that card's substitute is suggested first.
