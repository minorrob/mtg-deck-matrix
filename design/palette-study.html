CrankMagic · Interface colour · September 2026

Three ways out of one blue.

Every surface in CrankMagic today is a shade of the same navy — ground, panel, table row and text field all within about eight points of lightness of each other. That is why the Deck Lab's three sections read as one block, and why a field you can type into recedes behind the card holding it. The fix is not more blue. It is planes and a second colour. Below, the same four app surfaces rendered three times, changing nothing but six tokens.

What is broken

Inputs are filled #141824 against a #191b24 panel — darker than the surface they sit on, so controls sink instead of lift.

What each must do

Separate three planes; carry one action colour; carry one decision colour that is not the action colour; leave the five mana pips legible.

How to read this

The specimen markup is identical in all three. Only the six tokens differ, so any difference you see is the palette and nothing else.

01

Deep Field

Least risk · keeps the brand

The blue stays; it just stops being the only thing. Three genuinely separated planes, a warm amber reserved for the one decision you owe the app right now, and controls that sit above their panel rather than below it. Nothing in the existing wordmark, the mist animation, or the mana pips has to change.

Guild Slate

Most Magic · card colours lead

Take the hue out of the interface entirely. A neutral graphite ground means the five mana pips become the only saturated things on the screen — so colour in this app always means a card's colour, never a chrome decision. The action colour is the gold of a card border, which is also the only warm note the chrome is allowed.

Twilight Archive

Most character · furthest from today

A plum-ink ground and a teal action colour. This is the one that stops looking like a dashboard: the app reads as a spellbook you are keeping notes in, which is closer to what CrankMagic actually is than a metrics console. Teal is far enough from every mana colour that an action can never be mistaken for a card property.

What I would do

The recommendation

Deep Field, shipped now. It is what is already in the branch: the three planes and the amber Starting point block are live in Deck Lab today, and they fix the specific thing you noticed without asking the header animation, the card art, or the six deck pages to be revisited.

The one worth a real look

Guild Slate is the strongest idea here — "colour only ever means a card's colour" is a rule the whole app could be held to, and it would make the Shop and Collection tables read faster. It is a week, not an afternoon.

What is separable

The Required and Decision tokens are independent of the ground. The Mountain pip on required fields and the amber Starting point work under all three, so those can ship regardless of which ground you pick.
