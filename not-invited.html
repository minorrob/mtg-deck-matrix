You're not on the invite list yet

CrankMagic is invite-only for now. The address you signed in with isn't on its invite list, so a table's link can't seat you. A link never adds anyone to the list: only Rob does.

What to do

  1. Ask the person who sent you the link to ask Rob to add the address you signed in with. You can also write to admin@crankmagic.com.
  2. Once you're on the list, open the same link again. A link works for a day; if yours is older, ask the host for a new one.

Signed in with the wrong address?

Sign out, then open the link again and sign in with the address your invitation was sent to.
